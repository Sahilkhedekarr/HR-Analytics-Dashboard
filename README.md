# HR Analytics Dashboard
This Power BI dashboard provides a comprehensive analysis of employee attrition trends within an organization. With a dataset of 1,470 employees, the dashboard focuses on identifying key factors contributing to attrition across departments, age groups, job roles, salary brackets, and educational backgrounds.

# Key Insights
* Total Employees: 1,470

* Attrition Count: 237

* Attrition Rate: 16.1%

* Average Age: 37

* Average Salary: $6.5K

* Average Tenure: 7 years

# Attrition Breakdown
* By Education
 * Life Sciences: 38%

 * Medical: 27%

 * Marketing: 15%

 * Technical Degree: 14%

# By Age
* Most attrition happens in the 26-35 age group (49%).

# By Job Role
* Top roles with high attrition:

 * Laboratory Technician – 62

 * Sales Executive – 57

 * Research Scientist – 47

 * Sales Representative – 33

# By Salary Slab
* The majority of attrition (163 employees) is from the Upto 5K salary group.

# By Years at Company
* Highest attrition observed at 1 year mark (59 employees).

* Sharp drop in attrition with increased tenure, signaling retention improves over time.

# By Gender
* Male Attrition: 140

* Female Attrition: 79

# Dashboard Features
* Interactive filters for Department and Job Role.

* Clear visualizations with bar charts, donut charts, and timelines.

* Immediate insights into vulnerable employee segments.

* Drill-down capabilities for root cause analysis.

# Tools & Tech
* Power BI

* Data modeling, DAX, and custom visualizations
